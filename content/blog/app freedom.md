Generative LLM (Ai) ruins my mood. I just can't. Google is evil and I've been just coasting on their services for far too long. So I'm doing my fucking hardest to drop applications, software, etcetera that are fucking bad.


## Google Workspace
For many years, I've made use of google suite (now workspace). I like using my personal domain, I have a lot of files to store on the cloud, and I've been using gmail since it was invite only. However, I've been itching to get out of the cage for awhile.

Thanks to a couple articles I've read recently, I've picked up the motivation to actually do that.

## Docs and Sheets
I hate Microsoft and Google's system is right there. Much easier to share with other people. Quick and easy to make new files. Other alternatives exist for sure. Libre is opensource, but I keep hearing weird things about it. Scrivener is actually my go-to for writing long form works but I don't think it's ideal for notes, obviously not for making tables, etc.

Proton is an easy 1 to 1 comparison with Google services. I don't like all of it's services as they are, so I'm not just using Proton. I do think its replacement for Docs and Sheets is the easiest to get into though. I don't need anything fancy.

I've been off and on with Obsidian for notes. Markdown hasn't been as intuitive to me, but with Zola I've been using it more so I'm giving Obsidian another shot.

For Mail, I'm trying out Fastmail.

For cloud storage, I'm using Jottacloud. It's got great support for picture viewing, which is the most of what I keep looking at in my cloud. But it's also fine for everything else too! 

Not that Google Reader has existed for years, but I recently picked back up with Inoreader which is the closest clone we got. Happy to return to RSS readers.
