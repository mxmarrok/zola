## General Introduction

It's the name I use in the kink scene and it's also the name of my werewolf fursona.

I am trans nonbinary, demisexual, and pan/biromantic. Although you’ll mostly just see me refer to myself as queer. 💜

Love my countries requirements for appropriate healthcare.

Art is something I’m passionate about but have a hard time finding time to create and how to express myself. I'm carving time out, though. I've done a lot of work writing and I cannot wait to share what I come up with.
