I consider myself many things and that I have many interests. What I struggle with, is associating myself with anything that is part of communities I do not feel connected to.

What do I mean by that?

Well, for example. I'm really into leather. And (unrelated) I know I'm a furry. But in both cases, I don't really associated with anyone that does as well. I'm really bad at socializing. I had a fluke when I volunteered for PAX in 2010 and made a ton of friends that way - but I've done a shit job making friends amongst queers and kinksters.
