## Why Not?
I'm so fucking sick of not having a website. I knew this would be easy[^1] if I only found the right tools. I found the right tools.

## OK, but for real why?
Social media isn't so hot. I miss the blogosphere. I used to have a blog where I wrote about playing a druid in world of warcraft[^2]. That host faded away. I'm a creative type, and writing long form is good. Having a site where I can post whatever I want it wicked nice.
